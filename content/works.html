WORKS

Objective of the project

The soul purpose of the project was to reduce the patient-doctor interaction at the first stage where the skin cancer patients can detect the type of skin cancer they are suffering from or tend to have cancer symptoms via web application.

Proposed Architecture

For classification of type of skin cancer, we used deep learning models and convolutional neural networks (CNN). In this project we have applied ResNet50 pre-trained model for training of images. A very popular dataset HAM10000 was used to for training and testing purposes. After applying ResNet50 architecture a dropout layer with rate 0.5 and dense layer with 128 features with activation function as Relu along with l2 regularizer of value 0.02 was added. Again, a dropout layer at the rate of 0.5 was added including a dense layer with number of image labels and activation function as Softmax along with l2 regularizer with value 0.02 was added. The last dense layer act as an output layer for the prediction of classes. The layers of the model were not trained at first, rather weights were used. Again, from beginning to last 22 layers were trained for accurate prediction of classes.

Benefit to the society

  • To digitalize the physical detection of skin cancer at early stage.
  • Benefit the cancer patients during COVID-19 pandemic.
  • It will assist dermatologists in better analysis of the cancer and thus curing it before-hand.

Future Scope of the project

  • The map showing nearby hospitals and doctor can be implemented.
  • A domain name can be given to the website thus real time data can be evaluated.
